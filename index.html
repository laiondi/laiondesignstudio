// ABOUT

About me

The lore.

Anyway...:

I’m an industrial designer focused on furniture, product development and visual communication. I work across the entire design process — from early concepts and sketches to 3D modeling, rendering, prototyping and production.


My experience as a Production Manager at an interior design studio gave me a practical understanding of how ideas become real objects: coordinating projects, working with architects, clients and suppliers, solving production challenges and taking designs through to final delivery.


I’m particularly interested in the point where aesthetics, functionality and manufacturing meet. Whether I’m designing furniture, developing a product or creating its digital experience, I approach each project with the same goal: creating things that are clear, useful and visually distinctive.


I take design seriously. Myself, not so much.
